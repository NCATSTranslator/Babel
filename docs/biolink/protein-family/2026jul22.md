Generated by `docs/biolink/protein-family/scripts/count_protein_concept_levels.py` (see its
docstring) from the 2026jul22 build's Parquet exports, Protein Ontology categories in UberGraph, and
a local MeSH download. Read with [`../ProteinFamily.md`](../ProteinFamily.md).

## 1. Protein cliques by leader prefix and by whether a member names a sequence

A clique "has a sequence" when some member is a UniProtKB or ENSEMBL accession. Every other clique
is a concept that Babel types `biolink:Protein` without a sequence behind it: a UMLS CUI, a MeSH
descriptor or supplementary record, an NCIt concept, or a Protein Ontology term above the organism
level.

## 4. GeneFamily cliques that share a preferred name with a Protein or chemical clique

Of the 28,463 GeneFamily cliques (HGNC.FAMILY and PANTHER.FAMILY, never merged with each other),
18,606 have a preferred name that, ignoring case, is also the preferred name of a clique in Protein,
ChemicalEntity or SmallMolecule: 22,734 such pairs in all (`genefamily_label_collisions_top100.csv`
lists the HGNC families first, then the largest partner cliques; the full list is regenerated into
`--cache-dir`). A PANTHER subfamily is usually named after one gene product ("HEAT SHOCK PROTEIN
BETA-2"), so most of its collisions are with the PR gene-level term of the same name, not with a

## 5. A crude label heuristic over the Protein cliques not led by a sequence accession

UMLS T116 has no family signal, so the only cheap guess at which UMLS-led cliques are groupings is
their label: a word like *family*, a plural protein word, or a trailing *s*. This is reported as a
size estimate and nothing more; `label_heuristic_sample.csv` is a fixed random sample for checking
its precision by hand, which has not been done.

| leader prefix | family-word | plural-protein-word | ends-in-s | singular |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| UMLS | 197 | 2,672 | 4,637 | 141,865 |
| PR | 2,173 | 8 | 103 | 77,915 |
| MESH | 3 | 64 | 70 | 2,412 |
