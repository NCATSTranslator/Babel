# Protein families: how should Babel, NodeNorm, NameRes, Biolink and the UI model them?

A discussion document for the Translator developers, user-facing teams and data modelers, written
from the Babel side. It lays out what the sources say, the decisions the group has to make, eight
options with their consequences, and the Babel maintainers' recommendation. Nothing here is
implemented; the numbers come from the 2026jul22 build and are regenerated by
[`protein-family/scripts/count_protein_concept_levels.py`](protein-family/scripts/count_protein_concept_levels.py)
into [`protein-family/2026jul22.md`](protein-family/2026jul22.md).

Issues: [Babel #1170] "Protein Families under Proteins", [Feedback #1429] (private) "Cannot search
for Claudins", and [Babel #849], the "new new plan" for the protein/chemical/drug overlap that any
answer here has to fit.

## The trigger

A user typed "Claudins" into the Translator UI's Pathfinder autocomplete and got nothing. Name
Resolver knows two concepts with that exact label:

| CURIE | NameRes type | clique (2026jul22) |
|---|---|---|
| [`UMLS:C2718006`](http://identifiers.org/umls/C2718006) "Claudins" | `biolink:Protein` | `UMLS:C2718006`, [`MESH:D057167`](http://id.nlm.nih.gov/mesh/D057167) "Claudins" |
| [`HGNC.FAMILY:488`](http://identifiers.org/hgnc.family/488) "Claudins" | `biolink:GeneFamily` | `HGNC.FAMILY:488` alone |

The UI's autocomplete allows nine types (Drug, ChemicalEntity, Disease, Gene, SmallMolecule,
PhenotypicFeature, BiologicalProcess, AnatomicalEntity, CellLine) and excludes every `UMLS`-led
result, so neither appears. The UI team's reading of this (in #1429): the UMLS concept is wrongly
typed `Protein`, should be a family, and should be in one clique with the HGNC one, or users will
see two same-named options with no way to tell them apart. They also noted "Lipoproteins"
([`UMLS:C0023820`](http://identifiers.org/umls/C0023820)) typed `Protein` beside
[`CHEBI:6495`](http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CHEBI_6495) "lipoprotein" typed `ChemicalEntity`, and
asked for PFAM identifiers.

[Babel #1170] generalises the report: UMLS's semantic type T116 "Amino Acid, Peptide, or Protein"
covers "lipoproteins" and "claudins" as readily as one protein, Biolink has a `ProteinFamily` class
for these, and Biolink is inconsistent about what a family *is*. A gene or protein family is a
grouping that genes or proteins are `part_of` or `member_of`; a chemical family follows ChEBI, where
the family is itself a chemical and the members are `subclass_of` it. That matters because [#849]
wants the same concept to be reachable as both a chemical and a protein.

The four worked examples used throughout, as NodeNorm serves them from 2026jul22 (before PR #1124
took effect; the MeSH duplicates it fixes are marked):

| concept | what NodeNorm serves | the problem |
|---|---|---|
| Claudins | `UMLS:C2718006` + `MESH:D057167`, type `Protein`; `HGNC.FAMILY:488`, type `GeneFamily` | a family typed as one protein; two entries for one name |
| Lipoproteins | `UMLS:C0023820` + `NCIT:C20029` + `MESH:D008074`, type `Protein`; `CHEBI:6495` + `MESH:D008074` + `KEGG.COMPOUND:C01834`, type `ChemicalEntity` | the descriptor leads two cliques (#1124 resolves it, but `D008074` is cross-listed under Lipids D10 and Proteins D12, so which wins needs a stated rule, #730/#1123) |
| Hemoglobin | [`UMLS:C0019046`](http://identifiers.org/umls/C0019046) + `NCIT:C16676` + [`MESH:D006454`](http://id.nlm.nih.gov/mesh/D006454) "Hemoglobins" + `DRUGBANK:DB04945`, type `Protein`; [`CHEBI:35143`](http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CHEBI_35143) "hemoglobin" + `MESH:D006454`, type `ChemicalEntity`; [`UniProtKB:P68871`](http://purl.uniprot.org/uniprot/P68871) "Hemoglobin subunit beta" alone with its PR and ENSEMBL ids | #849's model case: the family-level concept is also a drug, and the human subunit is a separate clique with no link to it |
| Insulin | [`UMLS:C0021641`](http://identifiers.org/umls/C0021641) + [`MESH:D007328`](http://id.nlm.nih.gov/mesh/D007328), type `Protein`; [`CHEBI:5931`](http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CHEBI_5931) "insulin human" + 25 drug/chemical ids including `MESH:D007328`, type `SmallMolecule` | not a family at all; a species-neutral gene product that is also a drug |

## Three problems, not one

The thread mixes three questions, and several apparent disagreements in #1429 are about which one
is being answered.

1. **Findability.** The autocomplete's type allow-list hides `GeneFamily`, `ProteinFamily` *and*
   `Protein`; its UMLS exclusion hides the 149,371 Protein cliques that a UMLS CUI leads. No change
   in Babel fixes this. Every option below assumes the UI adds family types and either stops
   excluding `UMLS` wholesale or Babel stops leading family cliques with a CUI.
2. **Duplication.** Two entries with the same label. The options differ in whether they *merge* the
   two (one clique), *conflate* them (a toggle, as GeneProtein does), *link* them (an edge) or only
   *re-type* one. Re-typing alone, which is what pure #849 does, leaves two entries with different
   type badges.
3. **Semantics.** Is "Claudins" a class of molecules, which claudin-1 is a `subclass_of` (the
   ontology school, ChEBI and the Protein Ontology), or a collection, which claudin-1 is a
   `member_of` (the database school, HGNC and PANTHER)? And at which level of specificity
   does #849's "one fuzzy ChemicalEntity" sit? Biolink issues [#943] and [#950] are open on exactly
   these.

## What the sources actually encode

Babel already downloads most of the information a family model needs; it ignores it.

### The Protein compendium is mostly not sequences, by clique count

| leader prefix | cliques | without a UniProtKB or ENSEMBL member |
|---|---:|---:|
| UniProtKB | 149,881,839 | 0 |
| ENSEMBL | 3,052,956 | 0 |
| UMLS | 149,371 | 149,371 |
| PR | 80,199 | 80,199 |
| MESH | 2,549 | 2,549 |

The 232,119 cliques not led by a sequence accession have no sequence identifier in them at all.
Among the MeSH identifiers in the Protein compendium, descriptors (`MESH:D...`) sit in cliques
without a sequence 5,307 times and with one 40 times; supplementary records (`MESH:C...`, "Hbb
protein, mouse") split 89,843 to 12,998. MeSH's own structure is the point: a descriptor is a
grouping and a supplementary record is usually one protein. Section 1 of the generated report has
the full table, including the 152,210 UMLS and 6,574 NCIt identifiers in cliques without a sequence.

### The Protein Ontology says which level every term is

Every PR term carries `Category=<level>.` in its comment (counts from UberGraph):

| category | terms | meaning | example |
|---|---:|---|---|
| organism-gene | 88,779 | one gene's product in one species | `PR:P68871` (= UniProtKB:P68871) |
| organism-sequence | 69,082 | one isoform in one species | |
| organism-modification | 39,917 | one modified form in one species | |
| gene | 23,967 | a gene's product, any species | [`PR:000005537`](http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/PR_000005537) "claudin-1" |
| sequence / modification | 8,067 / 4,968 | species-neutral isoforms and modified forms | |
| family | 525 | a family, any species | [`PR:000050467`](http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/PR_000050467) "hemoglobin subunit", [`PR:000045358`](http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/PR_000045358) "insulin family protein", [`PR:000003262`](http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/PR_000003262) "collagen alpha chain" |
| organism-family | 43 | a family in one species | |
| complex / organism-complex | 233 / 505 | complexes | |
| seqgroup, union, external, ... | ~350 | other groupings | |

Babel ingests every subclass of `PR:000000001` as `biolink:Protein` (`protein.write_pr_ids`); the
80,199 PR-led cliques are the gene-, family- and species-level terms that no UniProtKB accession
reaches. Of the 568 family-level terms, 567 are singleton cliques in 2026jul22 and one is absent
(section 2 of the generated report): PR's family terms carry no xref Babel uses, so any link from
them to a UMLS, MeSH or HGNC family has to be made by label or by shared membership.

### MeSH descriptors are groupings; its records and tree carry membership

`D12.776` "Proteins" has 3,851 descriptors. [`MESH:D057167`](http://id.nlm.nih.gov/mesh/D057167)
"Claudins" has five narrower descriptors (Claudin-1 and so on) and 39 supplementary records mapped
to it ("Cldn1 protein, mouse"); UMLS carries the same structure as `CHD` and `RN mapped_to` rows in
MRREL. Section 3 of the generated report counts both for every descriptor under D12.776: 3,040 of
the 3,851 have two or more records mapped to them and 336 have none, and 3,818 of them lead or
belong to a Protein clique with no sequence in it (31 sit in a clique with one). That is the
evidence for "descriptor = grouping, record = one protein". Cross-listed descriptors are the
awkward case: `D008074` "Lipoproteins" is under both D10.532 (Lipids) and D12.776.521 (Proteins);
`D007328` "Insulin" is under D06 (Hormones) and D12.644 (Peptides), not under D12.776 at all.

### UMLS has no family signal; NCIt names families explicitly

T116 covers 167,663 CUIs and UMLS has no semantic type for a family. NCIt, as carried in UMLS,
does say so in the name: [`NCIT:C17274`](http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/NCIT_C17274) "Bone
Morphogenetic Protein Family" (`UMLS:C0053932`), "BRCA Gene Family", "Retinol Binding Protein
Family", all typed T116. NCIt also carries `conceptual_part_of` (3,585 rows) and
`gene_product_is_physical_part_of` (837) relations in MRREL.

### HGNC and PANTHER ship membership; a KP already serves it

Babel downloads HGNC's `family.csv` (names) but not the `gene_has_family.csv` and `hierarchy.csv`
beside it (gene membership and family hierarchy; both exist). PANTHER's downloaded file is the human
sequence-classification table, which already maps each UniProtKB accession to its family and
subfamily. The GeneFamily compendium (`genefamily.py`) is HGNC.FAMILY and PANTHER.FAMILY identifiers
with no concords, so the two are never merged even when they name the same family. Section 4 of the
generated report counts GeneFamily names that are also a Protein or chemical clique's name: 154 of
the 2,323 HGNC families share their exact label with a Protein clique (`HGNC.FAMILY:488` with
`UMLS:C2718006` "Claudins", "Glycine receptors", "Ribosomal proteins"), which is the floor for the
"two Claudins" problem, since a label match misses "Hemoglobins" against "Hemoglobin subunits" and
PANTHER's `PTHR12002` "CLAUDIN" against both. PANTHER subfamilies are named after one gene product,
so their 16,575 collisions are mostly with the PR gene-level term of the same name, a different
problem.

RoboKOP (Automat) already answers a query on `HGNC.FAMILY:488` with 23 `biolink:has_part` edges to
the claudin genes, so family membership is served by at least one KP today.

### Biolink's `ProteinFamily` cannot be used as shipped

In Biolink 4.4.3 (the build) and 4.4.6 (latest): `biolink:ProteinFamily` is a `biological entity`
with the `gene grouping mixin` and `chemical entity or gene or gene product` mixins, but its
`id_prefixes` list is **empty**. Babel's `NodeFactory` drops every CURIE whose prefix is not
registered for the clique's class, so a `ProteinFamily` clique would ship with no identifiers
unless Babel passes `extra_prefixes`. It is not in `gene or gene product or gene family`, so
predicates and ARA logic written against that mixin exclude it. Its history (biolink [#755]) is that
"gene family" carried the alias "protein family" until `ProteinFamily` was added as a separate
class in late 2021, without prefixes, and it has not changed since.

`biolink:GeneFamily` has prefixes PANTHER.FAMILY, HGNC.FAMILY, FB, interpro, CATH, CDD, HAMAP, PFAM,
PIRSF, PRINTS, PRODOM, PROSITE, SMART, SUPFAM, TIGRFAM, CATH.SUPERFAMILY, RFAM, KEGG.ORTHOLOGY,
EGGNOG, COG: no UMLS, MESH or NCIT, and it mixes family databases with domain and motif databases
(PFAM, SMART, PROSITE, PRINTS, CDD), which are a different kind of thing (a domain is `part_of` a
protein; a protein is not a `member_of` a domain).

Neither class is a descendant of `Protein` or `Gene`, so a UI filter on those types hides families
whatever Babel does.

### NodeNorm already unions the types of a conflated node

When a conflation is on, NodeNorm types the conflated node with the most specific type of its first
identifier plus every member's types and ancestors ([`docs/Conflation.md`](../Conflation.md)). A
conflation joining a `ChemicalEntity` clique to a `GeneFamily` clique is therefore served as both,
and a `GeneFamily` filter finds it. This is the mechanism option G relies on.

## The decisions the group has to make

| | question | why it matters | today's de facto answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Is a protein family a **class** (`subclass_of`) or a **collection** (`member_of`)? | Fixes the predicate; decides whether ARAs' existing `subclass_of` expansion applies for free; decides whether a family can be the same node as a ChEBI class. | Biolink: gene/protein families are collections, chemical families are classes. |
| D2 | Which **level** is #849's fuzzy `ChemicalEntity`: the family (Claudins), the species-neutral gene product (claudin-1), or whatever UMLS/MeSH/ChEBI happen to have? | Insulin's fuzzy chemical is gene-product level; Hemoglobins' and Claudins' are family level. With no fixed level the fuzzy node cannot be reliably linked to a family node. | #849: whatever the source has. |
| D3 | Can a clique carry **two leaf categories** (`ChemicalEntity` and `ProteinFamily`)? | NodeNorm derives a clique's type list from one most-specific class; multi-category appears today only as a side effect of duplicates. | No, by design. |
| D4 | **Cross-listed MeSH** (Lipoproteins D10 + D12, Insulin D06 + D12): which pipeline wins? | #1124 moves a CUI to its descriptor's owner but leaves two-owner descriptors undecided; a family rule has to state the priority (chemicals > family > protein?). | 20 known duplicate leaders, no priority stated (#730, #1123). |
| D5 | A T116 concept that is also a **drug** (Hemoglobin + `DRUGBANK:DB04945`, Insulin)? | Under #849 these are `ChemicalEntity` and DrugChemical conflation takes the drug side; under a `ProteinFamily` type the drug side has nowhere to go. | `Protein`, which is wrong on any reading. |
| D6 | Gene family and protein family: **the same thing, or paired things**? | HGNC "gene groups" and NCIt "... Protein Family" are the same grouping seen from the gene and the protein side. If paired, a conflation analogous to GeneProtein is needed; if the same, one clique and one class. | Separate compendia, never merged. |
| D7 | **Who serves the edges**? | NodeNorm serves no edges. #849's KGX output goes to DINGO; a KP has to expose it before the UI can expand "Claudins" to CLDN1..CLDN25. RoboKOP has `has_part` edges for HGNC families already. | Undecided. |
| D8 | Are **domains** (PFAM, most of InterPro, SMART, PROSITE) families? | Biolink lists them under `GeneFamily`; the user asked for PFAM. A domain is `part_of` a protein, not a grouping of proteins. | Not ingested (#260 asks for InterPro). |
| D9 | What about the **other** non-sequence protein concepts: complexes (PR complex 233 + organism-complex 505; `MacromolecularComplex` exists), species-neutral gene products (PR gene 23,967), modified forms (PR modification 44,885), enzyme and receptor classes (T126/T192, MeSH D08.811, "Kinases"), the 89,843 MeSH records with no sequence id? | A family-only fix leaves most of the 232,119 non-UniProtKB Protein cliques exactly as they are. | All `Protein`. |

## Options

Shared assumptions: the UI adds `GeneFamily` (and `ProteinFamily` if it becomes usable) to its
allow-list, and either stops excluding the `UMLS` prefix or Babel stops leading family cliques with
a CUI. "Cheap" means days of Babel ingest or config work; "expensive" means a Biolink release plus
NodeNorm, KP and ARA migration.

### A. Families are the fuzzy ChemicalEntity (pure #849)

- **Babel.** One `ChemicalEntity` clique per protein concept as UMLS, MeSH, ChEBI, NCIt and
  DrugBank have it, family-level ones included. Claudins: `UMLS:C2718006` + `MESH:D057167` become
  `ChemicalEntity`. Lipoproteins: UMLS + NCIt + MeSH + `CHEBI:6495` become one `ChemicalEntity`
  (D4 answered: chemicals win). Hemoglobin: UMLS + MeSH + `CHEBI:35143` + DrugBank become one
  `ChemicalEntity`; `UniProtKB:P68871` and `P69905` stay `Protein` with edges to it. Insulin:
  `UMLS:C0021641` joins the `CHEBI:5931` clique and DrugChemical handles the drug side.
  HGNC.FAMILY and PANTHER stay `GeneFamily`. Babel emits KGX edges from each specific protein to its
  fuzzy chemical.
- **Biolink.** Nothing required.
- **NodeNorm, NameRes, UI, KPs.** NameRes still shows "Claudins" twice (`ChemicalEntity` and
  `GeneFamily`). KPs with edges on the 149,371 UMLS-led Protein cliques see them re-typed; that is
  #849's cost whether or not families are considered. TRAPI queries with category `Protein` stop
  matching these nodes; ARA `subclass_of` expansion works if the edges are `subclass_of` and a KP
  serves them (D7).
- **For.** No new types; consistent with how ChEBI treats chemical classes; cross-listed MeSH and
  drug-protein cases fall out (D4, D5); already agreed.
- **Against.** "Claudins is a chemical" reads wrongly to biologists and to the UI team; does not
  touch duplication; the fuzzy node's level is undefined (D2), so family-to-fuzzy links are
  heuristic; families with no UMLS, MeSH or ChEBI id (most PANTHER subfamilies, all of PFAM and
  InterPro) never get a fuzzy chemical, so two parallel worlds remain.
- **Risk.** #849 does not say where the 80,199 PR-led cliques go, and the Text Mining KP keys on
  PR identifiers at every level.
- **Cost.** Cheap in Babel (it is #849); the downstream cost is #849's.

### B. Babel produces `biolink:ProteinFamily` cliques

- **Babel.** A new `proteinfamily` compendium (new `PROTEIN_FAMILY` constant) built from PR terms
  with `Category=family` or `organism-family`; MeSH D12.776 descriptors that are not the single
  descriptor of one record (the D2 rule); the UMLS CUIs that follow those descriptors (make
  `proteinfamily` a MeSH-owning pipeline, with a stated priority over `protein` for D12.776 and
  below `chemicals` for cross-listed trees); NCIt "... Protein Family / Superfamily" concepts via
  UMLS; optionally PFAM and InterPro entries of type Family. Concords: PR xrefs, NCIT to UMLS, UMLS
  to MeSH, and label or membership matching to HGNC.FAMILY and PANTHER. Membership edges from PR
  `is_a`, MeSH tree numbers and record mappings, HGNC `gene_has_family.csv`, PANTHER classification.
- **Biolink.** Must add `id_prefixes` to `ProteinFamily` (at least UMLS, MESH, NCIT, PR, PFAM,
  interpro, PANTHER.FAMILY, HGNC.FAMILY), decide whether it joins
  `gene or gene product or gene family`, and settle the predicate (#943).
- **NodeNorm, NameRes, UI, KPs.** The UI adds `ProteinFamily`. Duplication remains unless B is
  combined with E1 or the GeneFamily and ProteinFamily cliques merge (D6). KPs that emit `Protein`
  edges for UMLS "Claudins" see them re-typed; smaller migration than A.
- **Examples.** Claudins becomes `ProteinFamily` [`UMLS:C2718006`, `MESH:D057167`], label-linked to
  `HGNC.FAMILY:488` (PR has claudin-1 at gene level but no claudin family term). Lipoproteins is
  ambiguous between a lipid-protein particle (ChEBI) and a family of proteins (MeSH D12.776.521);
  D4 decides, probably for chemicals, and the family loses the MeSH id. Hemoglobins: `MESH:D006454`
  is family level (PR:000050467 "hemoglobin subunit" is a PR family), but `UMLS:C0019046` carries
  `DRUGBANK:DB04945` (D5), so the clique has to split. Insulin is not a family and is untouched.
- **For.** What #1170 and the UI team asked for; makes PR's categories, HGNC/PANTHER membership and
  MeSH trees useful; gives PFAM and InterPro a home; `ProteinFamily` already carries the
  `chemical entity or gene or gene product` mixin, so most predicate domains admit it.
- **Against.** UMLS has no family signal, so classifying 149,371 CUIs rests on MeSH descriptor
  status, NCIt naming and plural heuristics of unknown precision (section 5 of the report: roughly
  5,000 to 8,000 UMLS-led cliques look plural or say "family"). Two grouping classes multiply D6.
  Conflicts with #849 wherever one MeSH descriptor would be both the fuzzy chemical and the family,
  unless D3 allows two categories.
- **Risk.** A Biolink release cycle; a partial migration where some families are `ProteinFamily`
  and the rest stay `Protein` or `ChemicalEntity` gives three "Claudins"-shaped entries, worse than
  today.
- **Cost.** Moderate in Babel (new compendium, MeSH-ownership priority, classifier); a Biolink
  change; moderate downstream.

### C. A three-level specificity ladder from PR's categories

- **Babel.** Classify every protein-ish identifier to one of three levels and type each level:
  (1) organism-sequence, organism-gene and organism-modification are `Protein` (UniProtKB,
  `PR:P68871`, ENSEMBL, MeSH records such as "HBB protein, human", and the CUIs that follow them);
  (2) species-neutral gene products (`PR:000005537` "claudin-1", `PR:000050026` "hemoglobin subunit
  beta", MeSH descriptors with a single-gene meaning, UMLS "Insulin") are the natural level for
  #849's fuzzy `ChemicalEntity` (D2 answered: fuzzy = gene product, species collapsed); (3) families
  (PR family, plural MeSH descriptors, HGNC, PANTHER, NCIt "Family") are `ProteinFamily` (or
  `GeneFamily`, D6). Edges: level 1 to level 2 (`subclass_of` or `part_of`, across species), level
  2 to level 3 (`member_of` or `subclass_of`), level 3 to level 3 (`subclass_of` for superfamilies;
  MeSH tree, PANTHER family/subfamily, HGNC hierarchy).
- **Biolink.** As B, plus a decision on whether level 2 is `Protein`, `ChemicalEntity` or an
  explicit "gene product class" (biolink #950). `Protein` today implicitly means one organism.
- **NodeNorm, NameRes, UI, KPs.** NameRes could rank level 2 first for singular names (what a user
  means by "hemoglobin") and level 3 for plurals; the UI shows a badge per level; the Text Mining
  KP's PR ids map cleanly.
- **Examples.** Claudins: level 3 = UMLS/MeSH/`HGNC.FAMILY:488`; level 2 = `PR:000005537`
  claudin-1 with its NCIt and UMLS; level 1 = `UniProtKB:O95832` and so on. Hemoglobin: level 3 =
  `PR:000050467`/`MESH:D006454`/HGNC; level 2 = `PR:000050026` (`CHEBI:35143` is the tetramer,
  arguably a complex, so the ladder does not fit it); level 1 = `P68871`. Insulin: level 2 =
  UMLS/MeSH/`CHEBI:5931` with DrugChemical for the drug side; level 3 = `PR:000045358` "insulin
  family protein". Lipoproteins: level 3 or chemical; D4 still needed.
- **For.** Principled; uses data Babel already ingests; gives #849's fuzzy node a defined level;
  makes species collapse explicit, which NodeNorm users have long asked for; matches #950's
  "specificity" framing.
- **Against.** Only PR, and partly MeSH (descriptor versus record), encode the level; UMLS and NCIt
  do not; complexes and modified forms do not fit a line; three nodes per protein concept triples
  NameRes candidates unless the UI groups them.
- **Risk.** Large: all 232,119 non-UniProtKB cliques need a level, and PR gene-level terms with
  NCIt/UMLS xrefs will pull level-2 cliques together with today's Protein cliques in ways that need
  a clique diff to see.
- **Cost.** Expensive, but C is the only option that answers D2 rather than postponing it.

### D. Leave families where they are but mark them; UI fixes only

- **Babel.** No clique changes. Add a per-clique annotation (a second category `ProteinFamily` in
  the compendium's type list, or a boolean property) computed from PR category, MeSH plural, NCIt
  name and HGNC/PANTHER label match. NodeNorm exposes it as an extra category (D3 = yes); NameRes
  indexes it as a type.
- **Biolink.** Nothing strictly; cleaner would be making `ProteinFamily` a mixin or adding a
  "grouping" slot so one class can carry it.
- **NodeNorm, NameRes, UI, KPs.** A UI filter on `ProteinFamily` finds the annotated cliques with
  no migration; KPs unaffected.
- **Examples.** The Claudins UMLS clique becomes `Protein` + `ProteinFamily`, still separate from
  `HGNC.FAMILY:488`. Lipoproteins, Hemoglobin and Insulin are unchanged: still typed `Protein`,
  still duplicated against ChEBI.
- **For.** Cheapest path with any semantic content; reversible; produces the classifier B, C and H
  need anyway.
- **Against.** Entrenches "a family is a `Protein`"; does nothing for #849, which re-types these
  cliques anyway; multi-category cliques are a known NodeNorm failure mode, not a feature.
- **Cost.** Cheap.

### E. Family as a conflation

Two flavours, both in the existing JSONL conflation format.

- **E1, family identity.** Conflate each GeneFamily clique with the protein-family clique it matches
  (UMLS, MeSH, NCIt or PR family), exactly as GeneProtein conflates an HGNC gene with its UniProtKB
  protein. With the flag on, "Claudins" is one entry with the union of both cliques' types. Cliques
  are untouched. Needs a matching rule: shared membership (HGNC and PANTHER members against MeSH
  records and PR `is_a`) where available, label otherwise.
- **E2, membership.** Conflate a family with all its members (Claudins + CLDN1..25 + their
  proteins). This is what "a toggle" would mean for the #1429 user, but it collapses a hierarchy (a
  PANTHER subfamily sits inside a family inside a superfamily, so one gene would belong to several
  conflations, which NodeNorm's one-conflated-clique-per-id model cannot hold) and it makes
  "claudin-3 treats X" answer a "Claudins" query.
- **Biolink.** Nothing for E1; E2 stretches conflation past identity.
- **NodeNorm, NameRes, UI, KPs.** NodeNorm adds a flag; NameRes needs it for ranking and
  de-duplication; the UI picks a default. ARAs treat conflated cliques as identity.
- **For.** E1 is cheap, uses existing machinery, directly addresses duplication, and combines with
  any of A, B, C or H. E2 is the only option that gives the UI family expansion without a KP
  serving edges (D7).
- **Against.** E1 does not change types (Claudins stays `Protein` or `ChemicalEntity`); E2 loses the
  hierarchy and asserts an identity that is false.
- **Cost.** E1 cheap; E2 moderate and risky.

### F. Nothing in Babel; UI filters, KPs for edges

- **UI.** Add `GeneFamily`; stop excluding `UMLS`. **KPs.** Members come from whatever KPs already
  hold (RoboKOP's HGNC `has_part` edges; PANTHER in some).
- **Examples.** Claudins shows twice (`Protein`, `GeneFamily`); Lipoproteins twice (after #1124 one
  of them loses the MeSH id); Hemoglobin twice; Insulin twice.
- **For.** Zero cost; shows the real volume of family queries before investing.
- **Against.** The user's complaint stands; and since #849 re-types these cliques anyway, "nothing"
  is really "wait for #849".

### G. A + E1 + membership edges: the #849-compatible minimum

- **Babel.** #849 as planned, plus a **GeneProteinFamily conflation** (E1) joining each GeneFamily
  clique to the fuzzy chemical it matches (`HGNC.FAMILY:488` with `UMLS:C2718006`), plus
  **membership edges** in the #849 KGX output from data already downloaded or one download away:
  PANTHER classification (UniProtKB to family and subfamily), HGNC `gene_has_family.csv` and
  `hierarchy.csv`, PR `is_a` under family terms, MeSH narrower descriptors and record mappings.
  Predicates per D1; the suggestion is `member_of` for a gene or protein in a family, `subclass_of`
  for family in superfamily and for a specific protein under its fuzzy chemical (ChEBI style).
- **Biolink.** None required. Optionally add MESH, UMLS and NCIT to `GeneFamily`'s prefixes so
  family-level ids could later move there without a new class.
- **Examples.** Claudins: `ChemicalEntity` [UMLS, MeSH] and `GeneFamily` `HGNC.FAMILY:488`,
  conflated into one NameRes entry typed both; edges CLDN1..25 `member_of` `HGNC.FAMILY:488`;
  UniProtKB claudins `subclass_of` the fuzzy chemical. Lipoproteins: one `ChemicalEntity` including
  `CHEBI:6495`. Hemoglobin: one `ChemicalEntity` with DrugBank; `P68871` and `P69905` `subclass_of`
  it and `member_of` the HGNC hemoglobin family. Insulin: as A.
- **For.** Fixes findability and duplication without a Biolink change; each piece is incremental
  and reversible (a conflation or an edge file can be switched off; a re-typed clique that KPs have
  cached cannot); produces the classifier and the edges that B, C and H would need if the group
  later wants a family class.
- **Against.** Family-level concepts are typed `ChemicalEntity`; the UI team will still object to
  the badge (mitigation: NameRes and the UI show the `GeneFamily` type that the conflation adds).
  PFAM and InterPro still need D8.
- **Cost.** Cheap to moderate.

### H. B for source-labelled families only, A for the rest

- **Babel.** A family compendium restricted to identifiers whose *source* says "family": PR
  `Category=family`, NCIt "... Family / Superfamily" via UMLS, HGNC.FAMILY, PANTHER.FAMILY, PFAM and
  InterPro entries of type Family. Everything else (plural MeSH descriptors, unmarked T116 CUIs)
  stays in the #849 fuzzy chemical. Merge GeneFamily into this compendium (D6 = the same thing),
  keeping `GeneFamily` as the class if Biolink will not fix `ProteinFamily`, since `GeneFamily`
  already lists PANTHER, HGNC, PFAM and InterPro.
- **Biolink.** Either fix `ProteinFamily`'s prefixes or formally make `GeneFamily` the gene and
  protein family class (restoring its 2021 alias). The second is cheaper and already in the model's
  history.
- **Examples.** Claudins: `UMLS:C2718006` has no NCIt family term, so it stays a fuzzy chemical,
  conflated (E1) with `HGNC.FAMILY:488`: the same outcome as G. "Bone Morphogenetic Protein
  Family" (`UMLS:C0053932`, `NCIT:C17274`) becomes a family clique merged with HGNC's BMP family.
- **For.** High precision (no plural heuristics); gives PFAM and InterPro a home; the smallest
  Biolink ask.
- **Against.** The family class covers only the explicitly labelled subset, so the same kind of
  concept lands in two classes depending on source, visibly; D4 and D5 still need rules.
- **Cost.** Moderate.

### Compatibility with #849

Issue #849 commits to three things: one fuzzy `ChemicalEntity` per protein concept; one `Protein`
per UniProtKB accession; edges with provenance in KGX. A, E, F and G respect all three. D is an
annotation on top. H carves source-labelled families out of the first commitment, acceptable if the
plan is read as "per non-family protein concept". B conflicts with the first commitment wherever a
MeSH descriptor or CUI is both the only fuzzy-chemical id and a family id, unless D3 permits two
categories or B defers to A for those. C changes what #849 means for plural MeSH descriptors (they
become `ProteinFamily`, not `ChemicalEntity`), a scope change that would have to be made in #849.

One gap every option inherits: the 80,199 PR-led cliques without a UniProtKB accession (23,967
gene-level, the rest mostly non-human organism-gene terms) and the 89,843 MeSH records without a
sequence id are *specific*, not fuzzy; "one fuzzy chemical" does not describe them. Only C has a
place for them.

## Recommendation from the Babel maintainers

**Now, independent of any modelling decision.**

- The UI adds `GeneFamily` to its allow-list and stops excluding the `UMLS` prefix wholesale (or
  excludes it only where a non-UMLS identifier leads the clique).
- Babel adds a PANTHER to HGNC.FAMILY concord to the GeneFamily compendium from shared membership
  (both files are downloaded or one download away). Today the two never merge, which is a
  duplication of its own and is overdue whatever else happens.

**In the next build cycle, option G.** Keep #849's typing: the family-level UMLS, MeSH and ChEBI
concepts *are* the fuzzy `ChemicalEntity`. For hemoglobin, claudins and collagen that fuzzy concept
is the family, and the level does not need deciding to ship. Add a **GeneProteinFamily conflation**
in the same JSONL mechanism as GeneProtein, joining each GeneFamily clique to the fuzzy clique it
matches, and emit **membership edges** in the #849 KGX output from PANTHER, HGNC, PR and MeSH, with
provenance. With the conflation on, NodeNorm serves "Claudins" as `ChemicalEntity` and `GeneFamily`
at once, the UI's `GeneFamily` filter finds it, and the user sees one entry. Everything in G can be
switched off; a re-typed clique that KPs have cached cannot. And G builds the family classifier and
the membership edges that B, C or H would need if the group later wants a family class.

**Predicates to propose** (D1): `subclass_of` from a UniProtKB `Protein` to its fuzzy chemical,
ChEBI style, so existing ARA subclass expansion works unchanged; `member_of` and `has_member` for a
gene or protein in a family; `subclass_of` for a family in a superfamily. RoboKOP uses `has_part`
today, and biolink #943 is where this has to be settled.

**One Biolink issue**, consolidating [#943] and [#950] with D1 and D2: either populate
`ProteinFamily`'s `id_prefixes` (UMLS, MESH, NCIT, PR, PFAM, interpro, PANTHER.FAMILY, HGNC.FAMILY)
and add it to `gene or gene product or gene family`, or formally make `GeneFamily` the gene and
protein family class (its 2021 alias) and add MESH, UMLS, NCIT and PR to it; and in either case
separate the domain and motif databases (PFAM, SMART, PROSITE, PRINTS, CDD) from the family ones.

**Later.** Revisit H (source-labelled families get the family class) once the Biolink class is
usable and the report's counts say how many identifiers qualify. Take up C (a species-neutral gene
product level as a first-class concept) only if the group wants species collapse as a product
feature; it is the right end state and the most work. B as written conflicts with #849 unless D3 is
answered yes.

## Evidence still to compute

The generated report covers the counts above. Before the group decides, it still needs:

- the precision of the plural heuristic, by hand-checking
  `protein-family/label_heuristic_sample.csv` (200 random UMLS- and MeSH-led Protein cliques, with
  an empty `is_a_grouping` column to fill in);
- KP exposure: for the main KPs (Automat/RoboKOP, Text Mining, SPOKE, Multiomics), how many edges
  touch a non-UniProtKB Protein clique, split by UMLS, MeSH and PR leader, and how many of those are
  family level: the migration cost of A, B and C, mostly already #849's;
- NameRes and UI query logs: how often autocomplete returns nothing, and how often the query is a
  family label;
- a priority rule for the 20 cross-listed MeSH leaders in
  `input_data/known_duplicate_clique_leaders.tsv` (D4).
