# Every pair here attaches a substance with no structure of its own (a biologic, polymer, mixture or conjugate) to
# the clique of a structure that substance is not. glom() runs with unique_prefixes=[INCHIKEY], so a
# structure-less record has no key to protect it and one bad xref is enough. The pairs were found with
# babel-inchikey-audit (docs/tools/InchikeyAudit.md) on Babel 2026jul22 and reviewed by hand: tier A components
# triaged "likely misattached" or "review" and judged wrong. Each block lists every concord edge that attached the
# component, so dropping them separates it cleanly. Categories:
#
#   misattached         the key is an unrelated substance
#   component           the key is one part of the substance (ADC payload, conjugate ligand, radiolabel, chelator)
#   polymer -> monomer  the key is the polymer's bare monomer or a small oligomer, a substance in its own right
#   different but related substance   e.g. another species' protein, or a hormone's releasing hormone
